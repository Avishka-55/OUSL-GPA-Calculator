# 🎓 GPA Calculator for OUSL Students

🚀 **Just launched!** This is a simple and fast GPA Calculator web app built specifically for **B.Sc. Natural Science students** at the **Open University of Sri Lanka (OUSL)**.

## 💡 What It Does

This tool helps you **calculate your GPA instantly** by reading your result sheet (downloaded from MyOUSL). No manual typing, no headaches — just upload and go!

### ✅ Features

- 📄 **Upload your MyOUSL result sheet (XML/Excel)**  
- 🧮 **Automatically calculates GPA** based on GPA-contributing courses  
- ➕ **Add Non-GPA courses** (those that don’t affect your GPA)
- 🔐 100% **Privacy-friendly**: Everything runs in your browser.  
   - No data is uploaded, stored, or shared.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. Go to [MyOUSL](https://myousl.ou.ac.lk) and download your result sheet.
2. Upload the file to this app.
3. Boom 💥 Your GPA is instantly calculated and displayed.

---

## 🧬 Who Can Use This?

- 🎯 **B.Sc. Natural Science students** — designed just for you!
- 🧪 Students from **other OUSL faculties** may also use it if their course codes follow the same format.

### 🧾 Course Code Format

The app assumes course codes follow a pattern like: `ABC4301`

- In this format, the **5th character (3)** indicates the **credit value** of the course.

---

- 📊 You can **predict your GPA** by adding estimated results to the Excel file. Just make sure to **save the file as `.xlsx` format after editing** — otherwise, the app won't be able to read it correctly.
